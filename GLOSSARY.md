# survivor-dash

The league's Fantasy Survivor hub: the season dashboard (read from Kenny's scoring sheet) and the scheduler for the nights the league watches together.

## Survivor nights

**Survivor night**:
A get-together where the league watches the latest episodes together, usually two episodes every couple of weeks.
_Avoid_: viewing, viewing night, watch party

**Poll**:
The vote that picks when the next Survivor night happens. Only one poll is open at a time.

**Night**:
One choice offered in a poll: a day plus a start time, or a day with the time still to be decided.
_Avoid_: option, slot

**Voter**:
A league member who votes on Survivor nights. Not every team member is a voter.

**Voted**:
A voter has voted once they've answered every night in the poll. Someone who answered only some nights hasn't finished voting.
_Avoid_: partial vote counts as voted

**Team in**:
A team is in for a night when at least one of its voters says Yes to it.

**Bar**:
How many teams must be in for a night to qualify: 3 of 4.

**Clear winner**:
A night that clears the bar with more teams in than any other night.

**Starter**:
The voter who started the poll. If the poll doesn't settle itself, the starter picks the night.

**Commissioner**:
Kenny's role. The commissioner can do anything the starter can, on any poll.
_Avoid_: admin

**Set night**:
A Survivor night whose date has been chosen, either by a poll or added directly.
_Avoid_: locked night

**Situation**:
Where the next Survivor night stands right now (voting, waiting on a pick, no night worked, or set) and everything that follows from it: which nights lead, who has voted, who's coming, who may act.
_Avoid_: status (that's the saved state of a poll)
